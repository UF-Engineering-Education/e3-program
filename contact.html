Contact the program

The E3 Fellows Program is administered by the Department of Engineering Education in the Herbert Wertheim College of Engineering at the University of Florida.

Get in touch

We welcome inquiries from prospective fellows, faculty interested in mentoring, departments planning Visiting Assistant Instructional Professor appointments, and colleagues at other institutions building similar programs. For questions about the program, please send your inquiries to kapooramanpreet@ufl.edu.
