Mentor worksheets

Five forms for the mentoring year. Type into them on screen during the meeting, then print or save as a PDF — or print them blank and write on them. Each worksheet prints on its own, without the site around it.

1. Compact 2. Meeting record 3. Observation 4. Project planner 5. Growth review

Nothing you type here is saved. These forms do not send anything anywhere and are not stored in your browser — print or save the worksheet as a PDF before you close the tab. Keeping completed worksheets is up to you and your fellow; treat them as the confidential record of a mentoring relationship.

Worksheet 1

Mentoring compact

First meeting · mid-August, before classes

Fill this in together rather than sending it ahead. The value is in the conversation it forces — particularly the questions about feedback and confidentiality, which are the ones that cause trouble later when they are left assumed.

Worksheet 2

Biweekly meeting record

Every meeting · print a stack of these

One page per meeting, following the five-part shape. The part that matters most is the last box: actions with a name and a date against each. Start the next meeting by reading it back.

1. Check in 3 min

2. Since last time 5 min

3. Today’s focus 15 min

4. Decisions and actions 5 min

5. Close 2 min

Worksheet 3

Classroom observation protocol

Fall · one or two visits, with a conference before and a debrief after

Three parts, used in order. The point of part one is that the fellow chooses what you look for; the point of part two is that you record what happened rather than what you thought of it; the point of part three is that the fellow speaks first. Skipping part one is the usual reason an observation lands badly.

Part 1 · Before the visit

Completed together, a week or so ahead.

Part 2 · During the class

Every five to ten minutes, write down what the instructor is doing and what the students are doing. Record behaviour, not judgement — the judgements come later, and the record is what makes them discussable.

Growth review
