Faculty mentors

Every E3 Fellow is paired with an experienced faculty mentor who guides them through the full nine months — from course planning to the culminating scholarly teaching contribution.

The role

Individualized guidance, all year long.

Mentors meet biweekly with their fellow in preparation for and through the semester — discussing teaching challenges, classroom observations, professional growth, and career development — followed by monthly check-ins.

They also provide feedback on fellows’ instructional innovations and scholarly teaching projects, fostering reflective practice and continuous improvement. At the end of the year, mentors join the Department or Unit Chair in a final structured evaluation of the fellow’s overall growth and readiness as an engineering educator.

Read the mentorship component

What mentorship covers

  • Course planning and syllabus design
  • Classroom observation and instructional feedback
  • Assessment strategy and student engagement
  • Professional growth and career development
  • Instructional innovation and SoTL project guidance
  • Joint summative evaluation with the Department or Unit Chair

Cadence

How the mentoring year runs

Before the semester

Biweekly meetings begin ahead of Fall 2026, focused on course planning and preparation for the first teaching appointment.

Through the semester

Biweekly meetings continue alongside classroom observation and formative feedback on instruction, assessment, and engagement.

Into the spring

Monthly check-ins support the two-course load and the implementation and evaluation of the instructional innovation.
